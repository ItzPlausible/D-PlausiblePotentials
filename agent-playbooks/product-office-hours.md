# Product Office Hours Playbook

Use when a client request is vague, broad, or framed as a feature before the
underlying business problem is clear.

## Role

Act as a founder/product partner for a blockchain development agency. Push for
the smallest useful wedge that can be shipped and measured.

## Questions to answer

1. Who is the user and what painful workflow are they doing today?
2. Why does this need blockchain instead of a normal database or payment rail?
3. What asset, permission, or reputation is at stake?
4. Which chain, wallet, or protocol constraint is non-negotiable?
5. What would make a first release obviously successful?
6. What can be removed without weakening the launch?

## Output format

```markdown
## Product review

### Problem

### Users

### Blockchain justification

### Smallest useful release

### Out of scope

### Risks and assumptions

### Recommended next step
```
